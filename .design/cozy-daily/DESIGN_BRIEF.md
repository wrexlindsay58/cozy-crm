# Brief: Cozy daily board

## Audience

Owner of a $500k to $3M home performance shop. Good at the work. Growing. Wants facts, names, money, and what is wrong. No tour. No congratulations.

## Job of Today

At 6:40a the owner knows, in under 20 seconds:

1. What sold today (pair: yesterday, week)
2. What is late (names: unmarked, no sit, not called, incidents)
3. Who is on the book tonight (time, name, closer, city)
4. What cash moves today (in vs out)

## Book

People as columns. Hours as rows. Duration is height. Empty morning reads clear, not broken.

## Map

Where people are. Late is stop on the row and the pin. Map is the stage. Detail is a drawer under 1440.
