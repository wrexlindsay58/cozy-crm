# Pipeline chrome

The lead file and Inbox are the standard. Same depth, still simple. Later stages copy this chrome and only change the work.

Lead → Assessment → Opportunity → Job → Account.

Same house. Same thread. Same people. Different work. Talk follows the contact through every stage.

## What never changes

- Title, status, DND, Call / Text / Book / Create as icons with tooltips.
- Owner, followers, Follow / Transfer / Merge / Drop.
- 60/40 split. Conversation is always on.
- Tabs: Customer, Internal, Notes, Tags, Actions, History, Media, Form. Inbox also has Book.
- Tags tab is where tags and workflows get added or pulled. Not a pile of chips in the header.
- Book is history. Plus adds an event. Sales is the default on a lead. Set by is the user. Assigned and crew change with the event type. Notes and scope when the run needs them.
- Actions are tickets and tasks. Cards, not popups.
- History is a timeline. Internal is the shop. Customer is the house.
- Inbox: icon filter menus (who, channel, type, pipeline, extra filters). Star and unread stand alone. List is the house and the last message. Jump to the file.
- Menus and tooltips stay on screen. Chevrons sit in from the edge.
- Tap 44. Words drop to icons with tooltips when they clip.

## What each stage adds

| Stage | Left column work |
|---|---|
| Lead | Contact, source, interests, schedule, disposition |
| Assessment | Site packets, photos of the house, what we found |
| Opportunity | Options, proposal, take card |
| Job | Crew, schedule, holds, change orders, invoices |
| Account | Service, warranty, membership, new lead from the house |

Do not thin a later stage. If the lead has it, the job has it. The left column is what changes.

## Prior stages

On every file after lead, earlier files sit in expandable blocks under the stage work.

Closed by default. Open one, read it, keep working. Not a conversation tab.

## Shop voice

Confirmed, not set-no-run. Send a text. Ticket name. Landing form. Schedule, not book the run. No product language on the lead.
